# HouseKeep — Estrategia ASO

## Posicionamiento en tiendas

### Categoría
- **Principal:** House & Home (baja competencia de utilidades — dominada por inmobiliarias)
- **Secundaria:** Productivity

### Por qué House & Home:
- Las apps de utilidades del hogar son escasas en esta categoría
- Los top charts están llenos de Idealista, Fotocasa, smart home (Nuki, WiZ)
- Una app de "mantenimiento del hogar" puede destacar fácilmente
- En Productivity hay demasiada competencia (todolists, calendarios, notas)

## Descripción larga (Google Play EN — optimizada para keywords)

```
Stop forgetting about home maintenance. HouseKeep is your home's command center — tracking warranties, scheduling maintenance, and alerting you before documents expire.

⏰ SMART REMINDERS
Never miss a boiler service, filter change, or insurance renewal again. Set reminders days, weeks, or months in advance and get notified before it's too late.

🏠 HOME MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
Pre-built maintenance templates for your appliances: HVAC, water heater, washing machine, dishwasher, smoke detectors, and more. Just add your items and HouseKeep tells you when each one needs attention.

📋 WARRANTY TRACKER
Register your appliances with purchase date and warranty length. See at a glance which warranties are active, expiring soon, or already expired. Never lose track of a valid warranty again.

📄 DOCUMENT EXPIRY ALERTS
Track expiration dates for passports, ID cards, driver's licenses, vehicle inspections, insurance policies, and leases. Color-coded urgency: green (all good), yellow (expiring soon), red (urgent).

✨ SIMPLE & BEAUTIFUL
No bloat. No account required. No subscription. A clean, fast app that does one job perfectly — keeping your home organized.

🔓 UNLOCK EVERYTHING FOR A ONE-TIME PRICE
Free version includes 5 items and 3 documents. Upgrade once for unlimited items, documents, multiple homes, home screen widget, PDF export, and pro maintenance templates.

Perfect for homeowners, renters, families, and anyone who wants their home under control without spreadsheets or sticky notes.
```

## Screenshots (capturas)

### Orden recomendado (6 capturas)

1. **Hero/Overview** — Dashboard con 3 próximos eventos, diseño limpio
   - Caption: "Your home, always under control" / "Tu casa, siempre al día"

2. **Items** — Lista de electrodomésticos con iconos de categoría y badge de garantía
   - Caption: "Track every appliance & warranty" / "Controla cada electrodoméstico"

3. **Maintenance** — Calendario/timeline de mantenimientos con semáforo
   - Caption: "Never forget a service again" / "Nunca olvides un mantenimiento"

4. **Documents** — Lista de documentos con semáforo verde/amarillo/rojo
   - Caption: "Document expiry alerts" / "Alertas de caducidad"

5. **Notification** — Mockup de notificación push en pantalla de bloqueo
   - Caption: "Smart reminders, perfectly timed" / "Recordatorios en el momento justo"

6. **Premium** — Comparativa Free vs Pro con precio
   - Caption: "Unlock everything. Once. Forever." / "Desbloquea todo. Una vez. Para siempre."

### Estilo visual de screenshots:
- Fondo con gradiente suave (blanco cálido → crema)
- Device frame (iPhone 15 Pro / Pixel 8)
- Caption arriba del device en tipografía bold
- Sin cluttering — una idea por captura
- Colores de la app visibles (verde primary, semáforo)

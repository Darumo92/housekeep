## 2. The short version

**HouseKeep is local-first.** Everything you enter — appliances, maintenance schedules, documents, photos, notes — is stored **on your device only**. We do not run servers that store your data. We do not have an account system. We cannot read your data.

The only information that ever leaves your device:
- **Anonymous crash reports** (Firebase Crashlytics), if enabled by your OS settings.
- **Anonymous usage analytics** (Firebase Analytics), if enabled by your OS settings.
- **Purchase tokens** (Apple / Google / RevenueCat) when you buy the Pro upgrade — strictly to validate your purchase.

That is the entire list.

## 3. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally on your device, in HouseKeep's private app sandbox:

- Item details: name, category, brand, model, purchase date, warranty months, notes
- Photos you attach to items or documents
- Maintenance entries: name, interval, last completed date, reminder offsets
- Document details: name, type, expiry date, notes, attached photos
- App preferences: language, home type, onboarding completion flag, premium status flag

This data **does not leave your device** and cannot be accessed by us or third parties. Uninstalling HouseKeep permanently deletes it.

## 4. Data sent off device

### 4.1 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If your OS allows it, technical crash reports are sent to Firebase Crashlytics. These contain device model, OS version, app version, stack trace, and a randomly generated installation ID. They do **not** contain any of your items, documents, photos, or notes. Crash reports help us fix bugs.

Provider: Google Ireland Limited. [Privacy policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

### 4.2 Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

Anonymous interaction events (e.g. "paywall_opened", "item_added") are sent to Firebase Analytics if your OS allows it. These do **not** contain the content of your items or documents. They help us understand which features are used.

Provider: Google Ireland Limited.

### 4.3 In-app purchases (RevenueCat + Apple / Google)

When you purchase the Pro upgrade, your purchase token is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play and validated through RevenueCat to unlock Pro features. RevenueCat stores an anonymous installation ID and the purchase transaction.

Providers:
- Apple Inc. / Google LLC (transaction processing)
- RevenueCat, Inc. ([privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy))

We do **not** receive your name, email, payment card, or address from the stores. We receive only a Pro entitlement status.

## 5. Permissions we request

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| Camera | To photograph items, receipts, or documents you choose to attach |
| Photo library | To pick existing photos to attach |
| Notifications | To remind you about maintenance and document expiry |
| Storage (Android) | To save photos in HouseKeep's app folder |

You can revoke any permission at any time in your OS settings. HouseKeep will continue to work; only the related feature will be disabled.

## 6. Notifications

Notifications are **local notifications** scheduled by your device. We do **not** send push notifications from a server. The text of the notification (e.g. "Boiler service due in 7 days") is generated and stored locally.

## 7. Your rights under GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA

Because the App does not store your personal data on our servers, most data-subject rights apply directly through your device:

- **Right to access:** all your data is already accessible inside the App.
- **Right to deletion:** uninstall the App, or delete individual items inside it.
- **Right to portability:** *(Pro feature, coming soon: data export.)*
- **Right to object to processing:** disable Crashlytics / Analytics in your OS privacy settings.

For data we do process off-device (anonymous crash / analytics / purchase token), you may request information by emailing **darumo092@gmail.com**.

## 8. Children
